# Executive Coaching Page — Working Brief

Saved 13 Aug 2026. Working notes for the new `/` page positioning Ammar's
private yoga work as executive coaching. Not published anywhere.

---

## The idea

A private client (Tatjana von Stein, designer, co-founder of Sella Concept)
asked to be invoiced through her company rather than personally, labelled as
coaching. It genuinely is coaching: she's learning handstands, and that process
teaches progression, setbacks, frustration tolerance and sustained attention —
transferable to how she runs a business.

The angle: **executive coaching delivered through physical practice**, framed as
professional development a company can expense, rather than a wellness perk.

---

## Decided (13 Aug 2026) — draft built at `/coaching`

1. **Positioning** — H1 leads on the *honest gap*: "I'm not a coach. That's
   rather the point." Rationale: the research's headline finding is "lead with
   the business credential" (OTITỌ leads with McKinsey and prices as executive
   coaching; Evolve buries it and prices as breathwork). **Ammar has no business
   credential on the CV to lead with** — every certification is a yoga
   qualification and the profile line is "I came to teaching through practice,
   not through a plan." So that play is unavailable. Leading with the absence is
   the one position that stays honest and still reads as premium. It also
   satisfies the hard constraint by construction.

2. **Naming** — route is `/coaching`, not `/executive`. The bare word *coaching*
   is avoided in the H1 as planned, and appears in the meta description only.

3. **Client proof** — built with **two fictional placeholders** (Elena Marchetti,
   Hannah Reeve) and stock portraits, marked as invented in the *visible UI* as
   well as in code, so the page can be shown to someone without misleading them.
   Tatjana has still not been asked. Swapping her in is a single edit.

4. **Pricing** — **no published rate.** Follows the research's own
   counter-intuitive finding: the £500+/hr tier publishes nothing, and
   publishing at £70–150 is exactly what marks the lower tier. The page states
   that corporate and self-funded rates differ, both quoted on enquiry.

5. **Relationship to `/private-sessions`** — separate page, **not cross-linked in
   either direction**. `/private-sessions` keeps its £70–80 rate card: it
   converts individuals price-checking against other London teachers, and
   removing it would add friction to a funnel that works while gaining nothing.
   One click from an unpriced corporate page to a £70 rate card would reprice
   the offering instantly. Same person, two doors.

6. **Buyer** — the individual who expenses it. Page speaks to them throughout;
   the expensing section is written for that person, not for HR/L&D.

## Correction — the credentials premise was wrong (13 Aug 2026, later)

The research above concluded "lead with the business credential" and I concluded
Ammar had none, because the CV on this site has none. **That was an artefact of
his own earlier edit**: he had deliberately stripped his background to keep the
site yoga-focused.

The actual background: **chartered accountant**; Economics at Bristol + MSc
Finance; schooling at German Swiss International School (HK) and Eltham College
(UK); startup scene in Bangalore; corporate finance in London. He left by choice
— explicitly *not* via McKinsey or JP Morgan, and that accuracy matters to him.
Also: has lived in Latin American communities without electricity, i.e. genuine
range across the wealth spectrum.

**This does not change the H1**, but it changes the structure. Leading with the
CV would make it an executive-coaching page competing on credentials against
people holding coaching accreditations. Leading with the refusal and then showing
the CV four lines down is stronger: *he qualified, and left.* Implemented as a
credential band directly beneath the hero subhead.

### Second correction — the handstand was doing too much work

Ammar's note: the draft was "heavy handstand focused… I don't want to pigeon
hole this to handstands are the only way." Correct, and it was a real flaw —
the handstand appeared in the H1 subhead, in every one of the four transfer
items, and in the client quote. **The mechanism is difficulty under sustained
attention**, which asana, strength, breath and long holds all deliver. The
handstand now appears once, as one example among several. Do not let it creep
back into the H1 or the section headings.

## Research findings (25+ practitioner sites analysed)

### The core insight
The gap between what Ammar does and executive coaching is a **labelling and
payer problem, not a delivery problem.**

### The natural experiment — the single most useful finding
Two practitioners, near-identical credentials, opposite outcomes:

- **OTITỌ / Yewande Faloyin** — H1: *"a McKinsey consultant turned Certified
  Executive Coach"*; method described as *"we'll use yoga-based movement to
  unlock insight"* → ranks and prices as **executive coaching**
- **Evolve Breathwork** — founder was *"an equity partner at a global consulting
  firm"*, but H1 is *"The reset you need is right under your nose"* → ranks and
  prices as **breathwork**

→ **Lead with the business credential. The body is the method, never the
category.**

### Pricing comparables
- Private yoga London: £60–110/hr. London Handstand Academy: £150/month.
- Same 90 minutes as executive coaching: **£350–500**; £1,000+ at board level.
- Voyager Coaching: **£250 (SME) vs £500 (corporate)** for an identical 60 mins.
- Henley: corporate rates run **~2x self-funded** universally
  (~£125/hr self-funded vs ~£260/hr corporate).
- Group CEOs / main board: 40% of fees exceed £1,000/hr.
  91% of middle-manager fees at or below £500/hr.
- Founder retainers: first-time CEOs £1,200–2,000/mo; founders scaling past £5m
  £1,500–2,400/mo; PE-backed CEOs £2,000–3,500/mo.
- Published programme benchmarks (theone.coach): 6-month programme £3,590;
  monthly retainer of two 90-min sessions £600/mo.
- Typical programme length: **7–10 sessions**, not drop-ins.

### Counter-intuitive finding
**Publishing prices is itself a tier signal.** The £500+/hr tier publishes
nothing. Somatic and yoga practitioners publish freely at £70–150 — which is
exactly what marks them as the lower tier. If publishing at all: 90-minute
executive blocks at £350+, with an explicit corporate/self-funded split. Never a
yoga-style rate card.

### The expensing angle — almost entirely unoccupied
Across every UK body-led site examined, only **one** practitioner (Vix Anderton)
mentions employer funding at all, and her corporate uplift is 33% against a
market norm of 100%.

Templates worth copying: Ashley R.'s vendor-friction removal; Stratos's "ask for
annual invoicing."

⚠️ **HMRC "wholly and exclusively" test** — invoice and page wording should
describe leadership and performance outcomes, never "yoga" or "wellness."
Frame as "many clients expense this as professional development" rather than
making tax assertions. We are not qualified to give tax advice.

### SEO openings
- `executiveyoga.co.uk` now **fails DNS resolution** — an established
  competitor's SEO equity is decaying and available.
- "somatic coaching london" results are dominated by **practitioner training
  providers**, not client-facing services. Commercial intent is badly served —
  unusually clear run for a client-facing page.
- Audience searches "executive coach london", "burnout", "performance coaching"
  — *not* "yoga".

### Evidence caveats
- The claim "almost every UK exec coaching site uses enquiry-based pricing"
  could **not** be verified against a primary source. Don't cite the percentage.
- FMLM Buyer's Guide fee tables were rasterised and unextractable; only the
  narrative ("most programmes consist of between 7 and 10" sessions) is quoted.

## Build approach (agreed pattern, from /chefina)

- Draft page with dismissible orange draft banner, bottom-fixed
- Unsplash imagery (free commercial licence) — verify every URL returns a real
  JPEG before shipping; avoid `plus.unsplash.com/premium_photo-` (paid tier);
  **look at each image**, don't trust metadata alone
- Mark every invented claim; `DRAFT_NOTES` block at the foot of the file
- `noindex, nofollow` while draft
- Uses site's own Nav/Footer and palette (this is Ammar's own offering, unlike
  the Ina page)
- Existing palette: `#F4EFE6` cream, `#1C1410` near-black, `#785E3D` /
  `#6B5740` browns, `#EAE0CF` alt section bg
- Mobile: verify at a real 390px viewport via CDP, not `--window-size`
  (headless Chrome ignores it and renders at 485px)
- Add route to `src/App.jsx`, and to `ROUTES` in `scripts/prerender-meta.js`
  plus the `vercel.json` rewrite exclusion, for link previews
